    About this walk

    A 400 × 400 m reconstruction of Higashiyama, Kyoto, centred near Yasaka Pagoda (八坂の塔, Hōkan-ji). Walk the stone-paved slopes of Ninenzaka and Sannenzaka, climb their stairs, and watch the light change from golden hour to blue hour.

    Measured

    • Terrain: GSI DEM5A 5 m laser survey (bare earth), T.P. heights.
    • Buildings: PLATEAU 2025 LOD2 roofs/walls, facade colours sampled from PLATEAU textures.
    • Streets, widths, steps (step counts), walls/fences, trees: OpenStreetMap.
    • Ground surfaces (asphalt / granite paving / gravel): OSM surface tags + PLATEAU road areas.

    Artistic or inferred

    • Pagoda: hero model on the surveyed OSM/PLATEAU footprint, 38.8 m incl. sōrin (evidence-based; the popular 46 m figure is disputed). Fine carving detail is artistic.
    • Paper lanterns along Ninenzaka/Sannenzaka: artistic placement.
    • Falling leaves, sky, distant hills, fog, wet sheen and lighting: artistic.
    • Wall/fence heights without OSM tags and tree sizes: default values (inferred).
    • The traveller is an original procedural character.

    Controls

    Desktop: WASD / arrows to walk, Shift to run, drag to look, wheel to zoom. Touch: left thumb to walk, drag on the right to look, pinch to zoom, “Run” to toggle running.
