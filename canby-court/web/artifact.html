    About Canby Court

    A short cul-de-sac off 38th St in Bellingham, Washington: ten houses around a bulb, with 3905 Canby Ct at the top. Walk the street and around 3905, switch between a late-summer evening and a snowy winter afternoon, and in winter take the sled down the street — the neighbourhood's real sledding hill (134 m, 14.8 m drop).

    Measured / documented

    • Terrain: USGS 3DEP bare-earth DTM (0.5 m), NAVD88.
    • House footprints, street centreline and pavement: City of Bellingham GIS; storeys / year built from the assessor-derived building layer.
    • Roof form, ridge direction, roof colour, trees and hedges: observed on the 2022 / 2024 orthophotos; ground cover classified from the orthophotos and NAIP.
    • Sledding line: steepest descent on the DTM along Canby Ct.

    Inferred or artistic

    • Wall and ridge heights, roof pitch (inferred from storeys); facade colours, windows and doors (no evidence: artistic).
    • The three kickers, the run-out berm and the hay bales: artistic game additions.
    • Sky, clouds, snow, lighting, sound and the people: artistic.

    Controls

    Walk: WASD / arrows, Shift run, Space jump, drag to look, wheel zoom. Touch: left thumb walks, drag on the right to look, pinch to zoom.
    Sled: A / D (or ← →) steer, S brake, W paddle, Space pop / boost at the lip, 1 / 2 / 3 tricks in the air, R restart, Esc leave. Touch: left thumb steers, big buttons on the right.
